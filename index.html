About CodeBridge


Founded in 2022, CodeBridge is a non-profit organisation committed to bridging the digital divide in Johannesburg. We provide accessible coding bootcamps and digital literacy training to empower unemployed youth with practical, job-ready skills. Our vision is to create opportunities for young people to thrive in the growing technology sector.

Our Programmes


Web Development Bootcamp

A beginner-friendly course covering HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Learners build responsive websites and interactive features, preparing them for entry-level web developer roles.


Java Programming Fundamentals

An intermediate course focusing on Java syntax, object-oriented programming, and problem-solving. Learners gain skills to develop applications and prepare for advanced software development pathways.


Digital Literacy & Career Skills

A short course focusing on essential computer skills, online safety, and preparing CVs for tech-related job opportunities.
