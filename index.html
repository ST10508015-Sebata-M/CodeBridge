About CodeBridge

Founded in 2022, CodeBridge is a non-profit organisation committed to bridging the digital divide in Johannesburg. We provide accessible coding bootcamps and digital literacy training to empower unemployed youth with practical, job-ready skills. Our vision is to create opportunities for young people to thrive in the growing technology sector. Read our full story, mission and values.
