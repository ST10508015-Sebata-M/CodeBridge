Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains what personal information CodeBridge collects through this website, how it is used, and your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information We Collect

  • Full name and contact details (phone number and email address)
  • Learner application details (preferred centre, programme of interest, motivation)
  • Volunteer sign-up details (area of expertise, availability)
  • Sponsor/donor enquiry details (organisation name, contribution type)
  • Messages submitted through our Contact form
  • Donor name and email address for donation receipts

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to process programme applications, respond to enquiries, coordinate volunteer involvement, issue donation receipts, and keep you informed about CodeBridge's programmes and impact. We do not use your information for any purpose beyond what you submitted it for.

Sharing of Information

CodeBridge does not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Limited information may be shared with programme partners or sponsors strictly for reporting on programme outcomes, and only with your consent.

Data Security

All information submitted through this site is transmitted over a secure (HTTPS) connection. Form inputs are validated and sanitised to prevent malformed or malicious submissions.

Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. Should this change in future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Policy

CodeBridge may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out via our Contact page.
