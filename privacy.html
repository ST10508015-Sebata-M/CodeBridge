Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains what personal information CodeBridge collects through this website, how it is used, and your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Demonstration notice: CodeBridge is a fictional organisation created for a student assignment. The website's forms are sent to a testing service, so no real application, donation or message is processed.

  • Full name, phone number and email address (all forms)
  • Learner application details: preferred centre, programme of interest and an optional motivation
  • Volunteer sign-up details: area of mentoring, availability and hours per month
  • Sponsor enquiry details: programme, number of learners and an optional organisation name
  • Messages submitted through our Contact form
  • Donor name, email address and chosen donation amount
  • Your location, only if you choose to allow it when using the directions map

We use the information you provide to process programme applications, respond to enquiries, coordinate volunteer involvement, acknowledge donations, and keep you informed about CodeBridge's programmes and impact. We do not use your information for any purpose beyond what you submitted it for.

CodeBridge does not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Limited information may be shared with programme partners or sponsors strictly for reporting on programme outcomes, and only with your consent.

The directions map uses OpenStreetMap to display map tiles and the OSRM routing service to calculate routes. When you use it, the starting point and destination coordinates are sent to those services. Your location is only used if you allow your browser to share it.

When the website is hosted on HTTPS, information you submit is encrypted in transit. Form inputs are checked in your browser before they are sent, and anything you type is displayed back to you as plain text so that it cannot inject code into the page. The site also uses a Content Security Policy that restricts which sources of scripts, styles and network connections the browser will trust.

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

This website does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. Should this change in future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

CodeBridge may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out via our Contact page.
